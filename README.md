# hackto-2026-team-28
## **Track 1: Personal AI Agents to Organize Your Life.**

***PRIZE: $1000 CAD***

We’ve entered a world where everyone will have a team of AI agents working quietly in the background to help manage everyday life. Build AI agents that act like personal assistants, operators, coordinators, or companions across work, home, travel, health and family and everything in between. Think beyond chatbots. These agents should take action, manage complexity, remember context and help people reclaim their time and energy.

## **Track 2:** **Tangerine - AI Agents to Organize Your Financial Life.**

***PRIZE: $1000 CAD***

Money is one of the biggest sources of stress and complexity in everyday life. What would it look like if AI agents helped people better understand, organize, and optimize their financial world? Build AI agents that help individuals and families make smarter financial decisions, automate financial admin, reduce stress and improve financial confidence. These agents are proactive and identify financial risks, opportunities, wasteful spending, and anything in between. They could help with budgeting and spending, copilots, subscription and bill management, savings goal planners, debt payoff strategies, financial literacy, tax preparation and document organization


## 📊Final Judging Criteria

All hackathons need to incorporate a **LIVE DEMO** in the **3 min video** as well as if your team is shortlisted for the final judging.

- **👉 10 points:** Does this project solve a meaningful real-world problem in everyday life or personal organization? 
**Judges are looking for solutions that address genuine pain points across areas like productivity, home management, travel, family, health, scheduling, communication, or finances.**
- **👉 10 points:** How creative, delightful, or forward-thinking is the experience? 
**Judges are looking for bold ideas, memorable interactions, and agent experiences that feel surprisingly helpful, human, or magical.**
- **👉 10 points:** How thoughtfully and effectively does the project use AI agents? 
**Judges are looking for AI that goes beyond simple chat interfaces by taking action, maintaining context, coordinating tasks, making decisions, or enabling workflows that wouldn’t otherwise be possible.**
